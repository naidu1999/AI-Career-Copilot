  KARNA OS — SETUP GUIDE (for friends)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT YOU GET
  Your own private career assistant: upload your resume, scan live
  job boards, see matches ranked best-first, generate tailored
  resumes & cover letters, and track every application.
  Everything runs on YOUR computer — your data never leaves it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WINDOWS (10/11)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ONE-TIME (2 minutes):
   1. Install Python:  https://www.python.org/downloads/
      IMPORTANT: on the first screen tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
   2. Extract this ZIP anywhere (your Desktop is fine).

  EVERY TIME:
   3. Double-click  Start-Karna-OS.bat
      → First run installs everything automatically (1-3 minutes).
      → Your browser opens the app by itself.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MAC
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ONE-TIME (2 minutes):
   1. Install Python:  https://www.python.org/downloads/
      (version 3.12 — newer Macs already have a working python3)
   2. Extract this ZIP anywhere.
   3. In the extracted folder: right-click  Start-Karna-OS.command
      → Open. (First time only — macOS asks to confirm; click Open.
       If it says "Permission denied", open Terminal, drag the file
       in, press Enter, then double-click it again.)

  EVERY TIME:
   4. Double-click  Start-Karna-OS.command
      → Your browser opens the app by itself.
      → Keep the Terminal window open while you use it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
USING KARNA OS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Start screen → upload your resume (PDF or DOCX), pick your
    target job titles, and press "Scan job boards".
  • Matches → jobs ranked best-first. Filter, or use Quick apply
    to open many application forms at once.
  • Prepare → AI writes tailored resumes, cover letters and
    interview practice. Works with no signup — for better quality
    later, add a free Groq key in the .env file.
  • Tracker → every application, with follow-up reminders.
  • Profile → your skills, targets and contact details.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GOOD TO KNOW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Internet is needed for job scans and AI features only.
  • Your data lives in the "data" folder. Nothing is uploaded
    anywhere. Delete the folder = everything erased.
  • To update later: replace the folder contents, keep "data".
  • Job scans run while the app is open (daily at 8:00 if you
    leave it running) plus a catch-up backup at every start.

  Problems? First fix that works 90% of the time: delete the
  ".venv" folder and double-click the launcher again.

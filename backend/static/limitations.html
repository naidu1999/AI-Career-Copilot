Known limitations

Told plainly — because a tool you trust should tell you what it cannot do.

Matching

  • Scores are explainable heuristics — an audit of rules, not a hiring probability.
  • Remote eligibility, sponsorship language and salary bands may still need your human judgement.
  • Only postings published on documented endpoints are visible; some roles simply are not listed.

Job sources

  • Free APIs and ATS endpoints can change, throttle or stop without notice.
  • Rate limits mean a full scan can take time; sources cool down automatically.
  • Deduplication is strong but not perfect — occasional duplicates can slip through.

AI features

  • Optional. With no AI configured, matching, tracking and exports work fully.
  • Output quality varies between local and cloud models; review everything before use.
  • Generated documents are drafts grounded in your evidence — they can still contain errors.

Not provided, by design

  • No automatic application submission — you press send on the official portal.
  • No resume OCR (scanned-image resumes need text extraction first).
  • No scraping of portals that do not publish documented endpoints.
  • No uptime guarantee on a free, local-first stack.

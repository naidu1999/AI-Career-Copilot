Privacy policy

Karna OS v1.1 · Personal-local edition

The short version

Your resume, profile, evidence and application history are stored in a database on your own computer. Karna OS does not send your resume to job portals, does not submit applications, and does not include telemetry. There is no Karna OS server receiving your career data in personal mode.

What stays local

  • Your uploaded resume and parsed profile
  • Evidence entries, verification status and change history
  • Discovered job metadata, match scores and their reasons
  • Applications, notes, interview preparation and generated documents
  • Backups you create (written to the local backups folder you configure)

What can leave your machine, and only on your action

  • AI features you explicitly trigger (summarize, tailor, coach) send only the minimum text needed for that task to an AI provider you configured in .env. If you configure none, nothing is ever sent — matching and the rest of the app work fully offline.
  • Cloud mirror (off by default): if you enable it, your database replicates to a PostgreSQL service you own (for example your own Supabase project) so you can continue on another machine. Credentials live only in your local .env.
  • Job discovery reads public, documented JSON endpoints of job boards and company ATS sites. These requests contain no personal data — Karna fetches postings, it does not log in anywhere.

What Karna OS never does

  • Never uploads your resume to a job portal or applies on your behalf
  • Never sells, shares or analyzes your data
  • Never embeds tracking, analytics or third-party advertising

Hosted deployments

If an operator deploys Karna OS in hosted mode (multi-user), this document no longer describes their instance. Hosted operators must publish their own privacy policy covering: operator identity and contact, what is stored server-side, retention periods, subprocessors (database and AI providers), user deletion rights and the governing jurisdiction — before accepting public users.

Deleting your data

Export everything as JSON from Continuity & Data → Export, or delete your entire local database file (data/karna_os.db) at any time. Deletion is immediate and complete because the data was only ever yours.

This document describes the software as shipped. It is provided as a product template, not legal advice.

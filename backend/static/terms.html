Karna OS v1.1 · Personal-local edition

What Karna OS is

Karna OS is local software that helps you organize a job search: it reads public job postings from documented sources, scores them against a profile you control, helps you prepare evidence-based documents, and tracks your applications. You — not the software — make every decision and perform every submission.

What Karna OS is not

  • It is not an automatic applicant. It never submits forms, never creates accounts on job portals and never acts on a portal in your name.
  • It is not a guarantee of employment. Match scores are explainable heuristics, not hiring predictions.
  • It is not a source of legal, immigration or career advice.

Your responsibilities

  • Verify every job listing, generated document and prepared answer before using it — AI output can be wrong.
  • Comply with each job board's and employer's terms when accessing their postings and applying.
  • Only configure AI providers and cloud services you are authorized to use, at your own cost.
  • Keep your own backups of data you care about; verify restores before relying on them.

Availability and third parties

Free public job sources and free-tier AI services can change, throttle or disappear without notice. Karna OS depends on them as-is and cannot promise uninterrupted availability of any third-party source.
